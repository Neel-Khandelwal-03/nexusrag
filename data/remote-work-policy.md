# Skylark Dynamics Remote Work Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Effective 1 February 2026. Version 3.1. Skylark Dynamics is a fictional company; this document is sample data for NexusRAG.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who can work remotely at Skylark Dynamics, how hybrid and fully remote arrangements work, what equipment and allowances the company provides, and the security rules that apply outside the office. It aims to give teams flexibility while protecting the in-person collaboration that hardware development depends on.

## 2. Eligibility

- All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible for hybrid work.
- Roles that require hands-on work with hardware (assembly technicians, test pilots and lab engineers) are eligible for up to 1 remote day per week.
- Interns are expected in the office four days per week.
- Contractors follow the terms of their contract instead of this policy.

## 3. Work arrangements

### 3.1 Hybrid schedule

The default arrangement is hybrid: employees work at least 2 days per week in the office. Tuesdays and Thursdays are anchor days for all teams at the Denver headquarters, and team rituals such as planning and design reviews should be scheduled on those days. Teams may agree on a third office day, but it cannot be made mandatory for the whole team without department-head approval.

### 3.2 Fully remote

Fully remote work requires approval from both the department head and the VP of People, and each arrangement is reviewed every 6 months. Fully remote employees must live in a country where Skylark Dynamics has a legal entity: the United States, Canada, Germany or India. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires prior approval from People Operations because of tax and immigration rules.

### 3.3 Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the home time zone of the employee's team. Recurring meetings should be scheduled within core hours, and employees are expected to be reachable on chat during them. Outside core hours, employees plan their own schedules with their manager.

## 4. Equipment and stipends

| Item | Amount | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Home office setup stipend | 600 USD | One-time, after probation |
| Internet allowance | 50 USD | Monthly |
| Ergonomic chair reimbursement | Up to 400 USD | Every 3 years |
| Co-working space | Up to 250 USD | Monthly, fully remote employees only |

Laptops, monitors and docking stations are provided by IT and remain company property; they must be returned within 10 working days of leaving the company. Stipends are paid in local currency at the exchange rate on the first working day of the month, through the regular payroll.

## 5. Security requirements

- Company devices must use full-disk encryption and run the managed endpoint agent.
- Connect to internal systems only through the company VPN (GlobalConnect).
- Public Wi-Fi is allowed only with the VPN enabled.
- Printed documents classified as Confidential must not be taken outside the office.
- Report lost or stolen devices to security@skylark-dynamics.example within 1 hour.

Employees handling export-controlled flight-controller source code may only do so from a company office or an approved home setup that has passed a security review.

## 6. Travel and expenses

Travel to the office on anchor days is not reimbursed for hybrid employees. Fully remote employees are reimbursed for up to 4 trips per year to headquarters, including economy airfare and hotel costs of up to 180 USD per night. Expenses must be submitted in PeopleHub within 30 days of the trip.

## 7. Requesting a change

1. Discuss the proposed arrangement with your manager.
2. Submit the Remote Work Request form in the HR portal (PeopleHub).
3. Your manager approves or declines within 10 working days.
4. People Operations confirms the arrangement and updates your employment record.

## 8. Policy review

People Operations reviews this policy annually. Questions can be sent to people-ops@skylark-dynamics.example.
