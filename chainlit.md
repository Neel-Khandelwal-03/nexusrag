# NexusRAG

Ask questions about your documents and get **grounded answers with citations**.

- Every factual sentence cites the passage it came from. Click a `[n]` marker to see the exact source text, file, page and section.
- If your documents don't contain the answer, NexusRAG says so instead of guessing.

**Getting started:** index documents with `python -m nexusrag.ingest data/`, then ask a question. For example:

- *How long does the Aurora X1 battery last, and how long does it take to charge?*
- *What equipment stipends do remote employees get?*
- *What was Skylark Dynamics' revenue in Q2 2026?*

The sample documents describe **Skylark Dynamics**, a fictional drone company.
